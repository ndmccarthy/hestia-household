Hestia Household Scripts is a set of private automation scripts used only by the McCarthy household to manage their own email and calendar. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

Hestia Household Scripts accesses Gmail and Google Calendar data only for household members who sign in.

That information is used only to:

  • sort and label their own mail
  • send their own reminders
  • read their own calendar events

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data stays on the household’s private computer.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party access settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To ask a question about this policy, use the support email shown on the consent screen.
